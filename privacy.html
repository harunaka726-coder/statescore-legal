Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026.

StateScore keeps your log on this device. There is no account, and the app does not upload your states, notes, or photos to a server.

The log can include a score for each state, the season and year, a short note, an optional photo, and the time you saved that entry. The browser or the app stores that information locally.

Export downloads a file that contains the same log, including photos. Import replaces the log on this device with a file you choose. Delete all data, in Settings, erases the log and its photos from this device.

The Android app shows a banner ad along the bottom through Google AdMob. AdMob can receive an advertising ID, an IP address, and device details to choose and measure that ad. Scores, notes, and photos are not sent to AdMob or to the developer.

People in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked before an ad is requested. When that choice can be changed, Settings shows Ad privacy. Delete all data removes the log from this device. It does not erase information Google has already collected for advertising. The advertising ID can be reset in the device settings.

The copy opened in a browser does not show ads.
