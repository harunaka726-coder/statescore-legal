Terms of use

Last updated 11 October 2026.

StateScore is a personal log for travels in the United States. You choose the score for each state. The app does not check that you were there.

Your log is stored on the device you are using. Clearing the browser, removing the app, or using Delete all data removes it. Export a file if you want a copy.

Only add photos you have the right to use. A photo stays on this device unless you export it or share a map image that includes it.

State outlines come from Wikimedia Commons' Blank US Map by Heitordp, dedicated to the public domain under CC0.

The app is provided as it is, without a promise that a log will always be recoverable.
